📖 HOW TO PLAY

  • W A S D: Move your character.
  • Mouse: Aim toward the mouse.
  • Left Click / FIRE: Shoot enemies.
  • SPACE: Dash quickly.
  • Q: Use character ability.
  • START: Begin the game.
  • PAUSE: Stop the game temporarily.
  • RESUME: Continue the game.
  • RESTART: Start a new game.
  • XP: Defeat enemies to gain experience.
  • LEVEL: Level up when you collect enough XP.
  • WAVE: New waves become more difficult.
  • BOSS: Every 5 waves a powerful enemy appears.
  • ENERGY: Character skills consume energy.
